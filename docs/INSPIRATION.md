# Inspiration research

Researched 2026-11-10 from primary Adobe sources:

- Substance 3D Painter product page: https://www.adobe.com/products/substance3d/apps/painter.html
- Substance 3D features guide: https://www.adobe.com/products/substance3d/features.html

Painter's central workflow combines painting directly on meshes, layered texturing and real-time material feedback. Its professional scope includes advanced production formats and capabilities such as UDIMs, far beyond a small browser exercise.

Patina Lab reduces that workflow to one original UV sphere, six normal-compositing raster layers, linked 3D/UV painting, and image/project exports. A hand-authored procedural ceramic texture demonstrates the loop without borrowed artwork or proprietary presets. Roughness and metallic are global values used in an intentionally approximate shader, not a claim of physically based rendering or smart-material compatibility.
